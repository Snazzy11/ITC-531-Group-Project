It shows both jobs being queued, received, logged, and acknowledged.

compose build --quiet matching_worker image_worker

Each queue held one ready message while its worker was stopped. After restart,
both workers logged their test payloads. Each queue then showed 0 ready messages,
0 unacknowledged messages, and 1 consumer. Together with the successful worker
logs and the handlers acknowledging after logging, this shows both test jobs
were consumed and acknowledged. The workers still only perform placeholder logging.
