## Team Choice
AWS will be our choice of provider moving forward. Microsoft Azure Blob is our current, working backup option, however, should AWS fail to meet our needs we may need to consider a different backup provider considering that Azure does not support Amazon S3 and as such does not have endpoints compatible with it. AWS supports presigned uploads which will triviliaze the processs of allowing clients to upload images of lost/found items. Additionally, despite the term of the free tier being 6 months instead of 12, AWS has a better free tier than Microsoft Azure Blob. The credits that come with the free tier expire upon the end of the 6 month period in which the tier runs; the credits that come with Microsoft Azure's free tier expire 30 days after account creation. Should AWS drastically change their free tier we may want to consider other options.
